# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First public release.

- Theme-first freeform crosswords in the terminal, generated through a signed-in Claude or
  Codex CLI, or offline from software-engineering and Roman-history starter sets.
- Easy/medium/hard difficulty and quick/standard/long lengths, chosen independently.
- Grids constructed and validated in Python; provider output is parsed as data only.
- Puzzle shelf with resume, revisit, and active-play timing across sessions.
- Pencil mode, word checks, and letter reveals, recorded as assistance.
- Versioned SQLite library and JSON export.
- Layouts for 80 × 24 and larger terminals; `NO_COLOR` support.
